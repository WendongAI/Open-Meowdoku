# Meowdoku market coverage (research notes)

Open-Meowdoku is a **browser play hub** pointing to [guessingame.org/meowdoku](https://guessingame.org/meowdoku).  
The figures below describe the **commercial mobile title** often searched as “Meowdoku” (Oakever Games), used only to decide which languages we localize for. We are **not affiliated** with that app.

## Observed store coverage

| Source | Coverage signal |
|--------|-----------------|
| IndieTower (iOS) | Seen in **~79 countries** (charts / store pages) |
| IndieTower (Android) | Seen in **~83 countries** |
| CreativeScope / ad ops (Oct 2026) | Creatives across **~192 markets**; heavy **Japan** plan load |

## Where downloads concentrate (public estimates)

Cumulative / share notes from Sensor Tower–cited JP press:

- **United States** — often #1 by lifetime download share
- **Japan** — frequently #2 lifetime; repeatedly #1 JP monthly downloads (e.g. Jul / Aug / Oct 2026; ~700k JP downloads in Oct 2026 alone per Sensor Tower JP blog)
- **Russia** — cited among top share markets in mid-2026 JP summer report

Modelled last-30-day Android split (PlayStore Radar, illustrative — not publisher-reported):

| Market | Share (approx.) |
|--------|-----------------|
| United States | ~13% |
| Brazil | ~12% |
| Japan | ~5% |
| Türkiye | ~5% |

Chart / visibility examples also include: UK, Germany, Canada, France, Australia, South Korea, Mexico, Netherlands, Poland, Taiwan, Spain, etc.
